Protinex High Quality Protein
Nutritional Drink for Immunity & Strength
Zero Added Sugar Creamy Vanilla Powder
Protinex
is a vegetarian ready-to-serve beverage mix that helps bridge the gap of nutrients from the diet of Indian adults. Protinex Creamy Vanilla is an adult drink mix that may help build immunity and strength.
Key Ingredients:
Skimmed milk powder, maltodextrin, soy protein isolate, minerals (Di Calcium Phosphate, ferric pyrophosphate, zinc sulphate, potassium iodide), Nature identical flavouring substances, stabilizer (guar gum), vitamins (choline bitartrate, ascorbic acid, dl-alpha tocopheryl acetate, ergocalciferol, thiamine hydrochloride, calcium pantothenate, Riboflavin, pyridoxine hydrochloride, folic acid, d-biotin), Sweetener (Sucralose)
Key Benefits:
The New and Improved Protinex Creamy Vanilla provides nutrition to help support your strength
Protinex comes with high protein content (34g for every 100g) that helps in building stronger muscles and it delights you with its rich, creamy taste
Its Calcium + Vitamin D helps in building stronger bones and the 10 immuno-nutrients support immunity
The nutrients in Protinex provide energy for your day
The drink helps in maintaining lean and strong muscles
It aids in supporting bone growth and strength
Regular consumption of this product may help maintain your nutrition levels
This powder has immuno-boosters that support immune function and help improve overall health
It supports your overall health
Product Specifications And Features:
Product Form: Powder
Diet Type:
Vegetarian
Protein Per Serving:
34 gm of protein/ serving (100 gm)
Allergen Information:
Contains milk & soy, may contain peanut & wheat
Directions For Use:
Stir 3-4 heaped spoons (35 gm) of Proteinex in 200 ml of lukewarm or cold milk
May add sugar, mix until well dissolved and enjoy
It can also be mixed with cornflakes, porridge, or curd
Safety Information:
Read the label carefully before use
Store in a cool and dry place, away from sunlight
Keep out of reach of children
Don’t exceed the recommended dosage
